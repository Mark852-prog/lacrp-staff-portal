Before you punish anyone

Always run /player search first to check their punishment history. If they already have two kickable offenses within 3 hours, the next one escalates toward a temp ban. It does not go back to a warning.

4 Procedures

Hostage / Kidnapping RP permission

Check both of these before approving:

  • A good backstory and a sensible scenario
  • An Administrator is available to supervise the scene

Deny (for now) if you're the only staff on duty or calls are high. A good backstory alone is never enough.

Getting a player banned (as a Moderator)

  1. Collect clear evidence
  2. Submit a /ban-request
  3. An Administrator reviews it and approves or denies

Moderators cannot ban directly. Witness-only evidence needs 3 or more independent witnesses with no friendship or conflict of interest between them.

Server overload playbook

Flooded with mod calls, mass RDM/VDM, few staff online. In this order:

  1. Flag High Load status
  2. Call backup with /request staff
  3. Set a Peace Timer (:pt, max 600) and post the announcement

If it's disruptive behaviour rather than full criminal chaos, use the Priority Timer (:prty) instead.

Logging an approved roleplay

Run /roleplay log with:

  • Players involved
  • Location
  • Duration

A staff member misusing commands

If you see another Moderator use :to or :bring off duty with no reason, report it to Internal Affairs or a Supervisor. Don't sort it out peer-to-peer.

A kicked player comes back and does it again

  1. Check /player search for their history
  2. Two kickable offenses within 3 hours means the next one escalates toward a temp ban
